Our Story

Built by Creatives, for Brands That Move

Visio Studios started as a group of creatives who shared one vision — team up and help clients elevate their brand. What began as a passion project in January 2025 quickly grew into something bigger.

As structures were being built around us, we saw the opportunity to work with media agency owners in real estate and B2B companies looking to elevate their online presence. We went all in — from post-production edits to becoming a content marketing agency that handles everything from editing to full content strategy.

Today, we've delivered 1,000+ projects for clients both internationally and locally — and we're just getting started.

Our Code of Honor

This Is Who We Are.

Every member of VISIO lives by these five standards — no exceptions, no excuses.
01

Mission First. Team Second. Self Third.

We align with the bigger vision before personal preference. When priorities conflict, ask: "What serves the client and the team?" — that's always the answer.

02

Communicate Early, Communicate Clearly.

No assumptions. No surprises. If something is unclear, ask. If something is going wrong, say it before it becomes a problem. Over-communication beats silence every time.

03

Deliver Excellence — Always.

Every output carries the VISIO name. Every frame, every edit, every export. "Good enough" doesn't leave the studio. We follow through with discipline, meet every deadline, and finish what we start.

04

Take Full Ownership.

We own our results — the wins and the misses. No finger-pointing. When something goes wrong, we ask "What can I do to fix it?" not "Whose fault is it?"

05

Stay Coachable. Celebrate Progress.

We give and receive feedback without ego. The best work comes from honest critique, not comfortable silence. And when we win — big or small — we celebrate together.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does video editing cost at Visio Studios? +
Our per-video pricing starts at $25 for basic social media reels, $55 for video ads, $75 for real estate listing videos, and $85 for long-form content like podcasts and vlogs. We also offer monthly content marketing bundles starting at $699.
What types of videos does Visio Studios edit? +
We specialize in social media reels for Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts, video ads for Meta and YouTube, real estate listing and property tour videos, long-form content like podcasts and vlogs, and brand documentaries.
Does Visio Studios offer content marketing services? +
Yes. Our content marketing bundles include video editing, content strategy, social media scheduling, community management, and performance analytics. Packages range from the Brand Kickstart at $699/month to the Brand Powerhouse at $2,499/month.
How do I get started with Visio Studios? +
Book a free 30-minute strategy call through our website. We'll discuss your goals, recommend the right package, and get started within days.
What's the turnaround time for video edits? +
Turnaround depends on the package: social media reels are typically delivered within 24–48 hours, while long-form content and real estate videos take 3–5 business days. Rush delivery is available — just include "RUSH" in your message.
Do you work with clients outside the United States? +
Absolutely. Visio Studios serves clients worldwide. We're based in the Philippines and work with brands across the US, Canada, Europe, and beyond. Everything is done remotely with seamless communication.
