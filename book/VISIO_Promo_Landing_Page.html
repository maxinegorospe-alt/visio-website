Absolutely. You can move between tiers based on your needs. We'll work with you during your monthly creative sync to plan ahead for high-volume months (like launch seasons or events) and scale accordingly.

Every format your platforms need. Instagram Reels (9:16), TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn (1:1 and 16:9), YouTube long-form, Facebook, all from the same raw footage. One shoot, every platform covered.

We handle post-production, editing, color grading, captioning, QA, and delivery. You or your team handles the posting. We deliver content ready to publish with captions and platform-specific formatting included.

Every project includes your revision rounds, and every deliverable passes QA/QC before it reaches you. We also build your custom editing SOP during onboarding so your editor pod nails the direction from Day 1, not after rounds of back-and-forth.
What's the difference between month-to-month and a commitment?
Month-to-month gives you full flexibility, cancel anytime. A 3-month or 6-month commitment locks in a lower rate (up to 24% savings) and guarantees your editor pod stays dedicated to your brand. Longer commitments = bigger savings and deeper brand familiarity from your team.
How is VISIO different from hiring a freelance editor?
A freelancer is one person you have to find, vet, onboard, manage, and replace when they leave. VISIO is a system, a trained pod, a PM, QA, and a workflow that runs whether or not any single person is available. You're not hiring an editor. You're plugging into a post-production pipeline.
